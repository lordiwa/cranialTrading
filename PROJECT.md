# cranial-trading

## Description
Magic: The Gathering trading platform where players manage card collections, build decks and binders, find trade/sale matches, and message other traders.

## Target users
Spanish-speaking MTG players and traders who manage large card collections and trade or sell cards with each other

## Primary use cases
- data-entry, integration, collaboration

## Success criteria
Traders can manage 50k+ card collections smoothly on low-end devices, find trade matches, and complete trades or sales end-to-end; every interactive element is accessible to keyboard-only and screen-reader users

## Stack
- frontend_framework: vue3-typescript-vite-tailwind
- backend_framework: firebase-cloud-functions
- database: firestore
- web_deployment_target: firebase-hosting
